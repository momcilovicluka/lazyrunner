# Code of conduct

lazyrunner follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, and criticise ideas rather than people. Harassment, insults,
personal attacks and publishing someone's private information aren't welcome, whoever they come from.

To report something, contact the maintainer privately through the details on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/momcilovicluka). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer can edit or
remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules.
